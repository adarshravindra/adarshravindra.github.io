The problem

Companies selling through B2B channels and bulk orders manage catalogues ranging from 500 to well over 100,000 SKUs, updated constantly. Every prospect wants a catalogue tailored to what they actually buy, and every deal needs a quotation built off it.

That work was being done by hand — assembling custom catalogues in spreadsheets and decks, chasing product photography, then rebuilding quotations from scratch for each customer.

What I built

  • Digital asset management — a single place for all product detail, supporting 100,000+ SKUs and up to 1 TB of assets.
  • Custom catalogue builder — unlimited catalogues from a template library, with viewing filters and branded email sharing per customer.
  • One-click quotations — select products and generate a branded quotation in under a minute, sent through the same branded channel.
  • Built-in image tooling — background removal and frame presets, so merchandising did not depend on an external design step.

I built the end-to-end sales funnel from scratch alongside the product, which is how it reached 40+ enterprise clients within the first six months.

The acquisition

In 2023, IDfy acquired Salesroom. My co-founder Niranjan Naragund and I joined IDfy Labs as Product Managers — which is how I ended up building the Transaction Intelligence Platform and Site Scan there.

We faced challenges related to limited exposure to a particular industry and low funds, which made it difficult to scale our business on our own. Ultimately, we determined that an acquihire would provide the best opportunity for us to overcome these challenges and achieve our long-term goals. — Adarsh Ravindra, quoted in CNBC-TV18
