The problem

Every merchant onboarding onto a payment gateway has to be classified for risk and compliance — what line of business they are actually in, whether their website meets regulatory requirements, and whether they are selling anything restricted.

Doing that manually was slow and inconsistent, and it did not scale to the volume a gateway of this size onboards. Classification quality varied by reviewer, and the review step became the bottleneck in an otherwise automated funnel.

What I built

A decisioning model that takes a merchant's website link as its only input and returns a classification and risk verdict instantly.

  • MCC prediction — establishes line of business by returning the top two merchant category codes from a directory of 400+.
  • Website compliance checks — verifies liveness and redirection behaviour against IT Act requirements, and confirms the basics are present: home page, contact page, policy pages.
  • Risk checks — scans for restricted words and contraband items, evaluates business-specific context on the site, and flags potential impersonation.

The output feeds the gateway's onboarding decision directly, so a merchant can be classified and cleared without a human in the loop.

Outcome

Site Scan runs as production infrastructure for merchant onboarding at India's largest payment gateway, has classified over 4 million merchants, and operates at a $200K annual run rate while meeting Mastercard and Visa compliance standards.
