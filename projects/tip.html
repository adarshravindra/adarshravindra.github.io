The problem

Lenders were underwriting on tooling that had not kept pace with how borrowers actually transact. Traditional bank statement analysis extracts only a narrow slice of what a statement contains — keyword matching catches salary credits and ATM withdrawals, then gives up on everything else.

  • Underwriting leaned heavily on bureau data, overlooking the large majority of signal generated through day-to-day transactions.
  • Fraud detection relied on visual checks, while forgery tools grew more sophisticated and statements began reconciling perfectly on their face.
  • Template-based extraction broke whenever an issuing bank changed its statement format, producing false positives and unnecessary rejections.
  • Risk checks were static and identical for every lender, with no way to configure against a specific risk appetite or portfolio.

What I built

An intelligence engine that extracts, interprets, and transforms data across multiple sources into a decision-ready profile of a credit applicant — income, expenses, savings, obligations, fraud markers, and legal exposure.

  • Three sequential small language models replacing hard-coded keyword matching, so transactions are categorized by meaning rather than string match.
  • An LLM-supervised feedback loop that continuously trains and evaluates those models — the mechanism that carried accuracy to 94% within 8 months.
  • Champion-challenger and data-annotation processes, built with the ML and Data Analytics teams, to measure and monitor model performance in production rather than at launch only.
  • A configurable risk library so each lender could tune signals to its own risk appetite instead of accepting a fixed set of checks.
  • Modular delivery — statement collection, pre-check, analysis, and risk flags as separate modules that slot into an existing LOS/LMS without re-architecting it.

Patents

Two patents were filed out of the work on this platform — one covering the categorization system, one covering the model-training feedback loop.

Outcome

The platform became core infrastructure for how lenders assess risk on IDfy's stack. In deployment with a personal-loan fintech, it processed roughly 80,000 new-to-credit applications a month without underwriter involvement, improved funnel conversion 1.5x through modular implementation, and cut analysis cost by around 40% via selective signal subscription.
