LinkMe

Personal project · 2026 · In progress

A platform for students to find aspirational LinkedIn profiles and learn from aggregated insights drawn from people who started where they are now and reached where they want to go.

The idea

Career advice is abundant and generic. What students actually want is specific: someone who started from their exact starting point — same school, same first role, same background — and made it to the place they are aiming for.

LinkMe finds those people, aggregates the paths they took into insights rather than anecdotes, and surfaces the patterns that repeat.

The second use

It is also a way to find people on LinkedIn whose story overlaps with yours — the ones where a cold reachout has a materially higher chance of landing, because there is a real shared throughline to reference.
